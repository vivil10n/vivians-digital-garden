## what it is

A glossyhyper-feminine early to mid 2000s aesthetic built around celebrity culture, conspicuous luxury, paparazzi photography, rhinestones, designer logos and unapologetic excess!

CARI says that McBling as a reaction to the cleaner futurism of Y2K. Instead of looking technological or sleek, it leans into glamour and over the top femininity: hot pink, metallics, velour, tiny handbags, bedazzled accessories and everything made to look expensive or flashy or famousss

## what I like about it

- it is completely unafraid of being excessive
- femininity feels loud rather than delicate
- everything is glossy, decorative and slightly ridiculous
- celebrity culture becomes part of the visual language
- there is something fun about how obviously aspirational it is
- logos and branding are treated almost like decoration
- it feels specific to a time when pop culture was much more visibly mediated through magazines and paparazzi

## what it is

An educational aesthetic associated with late-1980s to late-1990s encyclopedias, teaching materials and early digital learning products. CARI describes it as combining classical, "timeless" typography with idealised imagery and stock-photo collage designed for quick comprehension. 

## what I like about it

- learning feels optimistic
- dense information still feels inviting
- educational design has a sense of wonder
- strange mix of seriousness and play

## recurring elements

- encyclopedic layouts
- classical typography
- stock-photo collage
- clean diagrams
- nature/science imagery
- educational icons
- highly legible composition

## where I see it

- school textbooks (shout out pearson science, i lowkey hated you but i appreciate you now)
- museum interactives
- educational CD-ROMs
- early edutainment software

## what it is

A 1970s–80s vision of the future shaped by bulky analogue technology, angular hardware, control panels, physical media and industrial sci-fi design. CARI describes it as closely associated with the period's futuristic presentation of technologies like cassette tapes.

## what I like about it

- technology feels physical and legible
- lots of buttons, switches and modular pieces
- optimistic futurism without everything being invisible or glassy
- machines look designed to be operated

## recurring elements

- chunky hardware
- grids and vents
- monochrome or muted industrial palettes
- glowing displays
- tactile controls
- modular interfaces
- analogue media

## where I see it

- old hi-fi equipment
- sci-fi control rooms
- retro industrial product design
- contemporary interfaces referencing physical hardware

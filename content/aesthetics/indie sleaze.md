## what it is

A messy, hedonistic mid to late 2000s aesthetic shaped by indie music, nightlife, MySpace, flash photography, American Apparel and the bloghaus scene

CARI describes it as a mix of indie culture, post-electroclash, 70s–80s revival references and the chaotic energy of the NYC party scene. It feels deliberately unpolished: sweaty clubs, overexposed photos, smudged makeup, graphic tees and badly lit digital cameras.

## what I like about it

- it feels lived-in rather than curated
- there is a sense of chaos and spontaneity
- flash photography makes everything feel immediate and intimate
- the graphics feel DIY, scrappy and personality-driven
- fashion looks styled but never too polished
- it captures a version of the internet that felt more social and less optimised

## recurring elements

- harsh direct flash photography
- grainy or low-resolution digital images
- candid party photos
- skinny jeans and oversized graphic tees
- leather jackets
- converse
- messy hair and smudged eyeliner
- shutter shades
- neon accents
- hand-drawn or blocky typography
- collage and DIY graphics
- MySpace layouts
- blog graphics
- gig posters and club flyers
- overexposure and motion blur
- deliberately awkward framing

## where I see it

- late 2000s party photography
- myspace
- indie blogs and Blogspot
- bloghaus
- electroclash
- American Apparel campaigns
- old tumblr

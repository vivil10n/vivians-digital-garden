## what it is
An internet horror aesthetic built around weirdly empty spaces that feel familiar yet slightly off.
The Backrooms started as this weird piece of online horror lore about “clipping” out of reality and ending up in an endless maze of buzzing fluorescent lights and damp carpet. From there it turned into its own whole world of levels, entities, found-footage videos, wikis and fan made stories.

Visually, it overlaps a lot with liminal spaces: empty office corridors, hotel hallways, malls, basements, waiting rooms and other places that feel familiar but strangely abandoned. What makes it feel different is the internet-lore side of it. The spaces are not just empty, they feel like part of a hidden world with rules you are not supposed to understand.

built from people collectively turning ordinary images into mythology.
## what I like about it

- ordinary spaces become unsettling without needing anything obviously scary
- repetition makes architecture feel infinite
- fluorescent lighting creates a very specific kind of discomfort
- it turns bland commercial interiors into something surreal
- the emptiness makes you imagine what should be there
- internet-native
- there is something compelling about spaces that feel remembered rather than real

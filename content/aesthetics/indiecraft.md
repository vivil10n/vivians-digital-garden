## what it is

A handmade aesthetic associated with the late 2000s and early 2010s indie scene, where craft, DIY culture, blogging, small-batch making and personal expression all overlap.

CARI frames it around the rise of handmade culture online, especially the visual world of Etsy, craft blogs, zines and indie makers. It feels warm, personal and intentionally imperfect, with visible traces of the person who made it.

## what I like about it

- it makes design feel personal and human
- imperfections are treated as part of the charm
- there is a strong sense of authorship
- handmade textures make things feel more tactile
- it values care and effort over polish
- it reminds me of early internet spaces where people made things for each other
- it feels creative without needing to look expensive or professional

## recurring elements

- hand-drawn lettering
- embroidery
- crochet and knitting
- paper cut-outs
- collage
- screen printing
- stamps
- handmade packaging
- visible stitching
- recycled or found materials
- muted pastels and earthy colours
- floral and folk-inspired motifs
- simple geometric patterns
- zines
- craft photography
- whimsy

## what it is

A bright, maximalist visual language drawn from Filipino pop culture in the late 1990s and early 2000s.

within teen magazines, celebrity notebooks, variety shows, music-video countdowns, mall fashion, beauty ads, school supplies, and early INTERNET culture.

i love how playful and unapologetically expressive it feels: glossy, colourful, camp, feminine, youthful.

## what I like about it

- it feels joyful without trying to be tasteful
- graphics are dense, colourful, and full of personality
- celebrity culture feels tactile and physical rather than algorithmic
- it reminds me of magazines, notebooks, stickers, and school supplies
- there is a kind of optimism in the styling
- nothing feels overly minimal or restrained
- it feels distinctly local rather than like generic global Y2K

## recurring elements

- bright pinks, blues, purples, lime greens, and metallics
- glossy or airbrushed photography
- heavily saturated studio backdrops
- outlined and shadowed typography
- bubbly, rounded, or decorative fonts
- stars, hearts, butterflies, flowers, sparkles
- sticker-like graphics
- layered text and image compositions
- magazine cut-outs and collage
- gel-pen doodles
- chunky borders
- gradients
- metallic or glitter effects
- celebrity portraits
- baby tees and low-rise denim
- butterfly clips and colourful hair accessories
- frosted makeup and glossy lips
- beaded jewellery
- platform shoes
- colourful school supplies and notebooks

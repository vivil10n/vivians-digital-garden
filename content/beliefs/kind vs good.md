“As I have said, the first thing is to be honest with yourself. You can never have an impact on society if you have not changed yourself... Great peacemakers are all people of integrity, of honesty, but humility.”

― Nelson Mandela

i believe it is important to do the best you can. when we are born, we are given a set of circumstances which we live by. what good is change when it is not born from within? so that it stays with you.

i often wonder what the difference between a kind and good person is. I believe that I am kind. being kind is easy, nice even, you get to feel good because you had made someone else feel good but to be a good person takes discipline, intelligence and understanding of a world bigger than yourself.

to be aware of your community, politics, how your small actions affect something e.g. "just because our oceans are rising and there's nothing we can do about it so i may as well not throw my rubbish away. what's the use i'm just one person" 

just because we feel small does not mean our collective action is small. try your best to not litter. reduce unnecessary consumption. eat well. stay healthy. love well. continue the cycle.

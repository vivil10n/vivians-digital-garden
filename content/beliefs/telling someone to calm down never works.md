when i was younger (and in an argument), i would often tell the other person to "calm down"...

now that i have grown up a little, i realise these are fighting words!

this realisation came to me via bob the drag queen during his analysis of the new love island season 8 reunion. where kayda and that other girl are fighting and she keeps telling them to calm down. what this really does is minimise the others emotions and make them feel like their emotions are too big. rather, you should acknowledge their emotions and make them feel seen. unless you want to be petty...

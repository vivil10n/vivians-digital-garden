A digital birthday letter I made for my friend Odel’s 21st.

We met while studying abroad in Tokyo and became close during the brief time we lived in the same city. When exchange ended, he returned to San Francisco and I came home to Sydney. A friendship that had once been part of everyday life was suddenly separated by an ocean.

I made this site to send him the words I could not give him in person 💌
